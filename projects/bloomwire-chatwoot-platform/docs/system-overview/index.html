01 One-line mental model

If you remember nothing else, remember these four equivalences. They explain how the two systems relate.

🌐 Bloomwire = SaaS business layer

Bloomwire is an omnichannel customer-service SaaS platform. It adds the business/control surface: tenants, plans, onboarding, analytics, permissions.

💬 Chatwoot = conversation engine inside Bloomwire

Chatwoot Community Edition is the base engine for inboxes, conversations, messages, contacts and channels. Bloomwire is built on top of it, not as a fork.

🏢 Chatwoot Account = Bloomwire Tenant / Company

Each Chatwoot account represents one company (one tenant). Dialog, Mobitel and Hutch are each their own account.

🧩 Bloomwire Business Profile = SaaS metadata for that company

A bloomwire_business_profiles row hangs off the account and stores SaaS metadata: industry, plan, status, onboarding. It never replaces the account.

Plain-language version: Bloomwire is the shop. Chatwoot is the engine inside the shop that actually moves the messages. Every company that signs up gets its own private engine (its account), and Bloomwire keeps a little index card (its business profile) describing that company.

02 Tenants — Dialog, Mobitel & Hutch

A tenant is one company living inside Bloomwire. Each tenant is a separate Chatwoot account with its own staff, inboxes and conversations. Tenants are isolated from each other.

03 Why there are multiple admins

"Admin" means different things at different layers. This is the most common source of confusion, so here is each layer, who it belongs to, and where it works.

👑 Bloomwire Platform Admin / Super Admin
  • Belongs to the Bloomwire team (the platform operators).
  • Uses the white /super_admin/... console.
  • Sees all tenants and manages all businesses.
  • Manages platform-level settings.
  • Should not be used by client agents.
👤 Business Owner / Business Admin
  • Belongs to one tenant / company.
  • Uses the black /app/accounts/:accountId/... workspace.
  • Sees only their own business.
  • Manages staff, inbox, channels, reports & business settings — depending on permissions.
👀 Supervisor
  • Belongs to one business.
  • Sees team-level conversations / reports depending on permissions.
  • Does not see platform admin pages.
🎧 Agent
  • Belongs to one business.
  • Handles conversations.
  • Sees only assigned / allowed inboxes & conversations.
  • Is not a platform admin.
🧑 Customer
  • External WhatsApp / Messenger / Instagram / etc. user.
  • Never logs into the Bloomwire dashboard.
  • Talks to the business through channels only.

04 White Console vs Black Workspace

There are two completely different UI areas. Knowing which one you are in tells you who it is for and what data it shows.

⚡ White Console

/super_admin/...

Bloomwire / Chatwoot platform admin area.

  • For platform operators (the Bloomwire team).
  • Spans all businesses / tenants.
  • Manages accounts, users, platform settings, and Bloomwire Businesses.
  • Built on the administrate gem (the classic light Super Admin UI).

🌑 Black Workspace

/app/accounts/:accountId/...

The business's daily workspace.

  • For owner / admin / supervisor / agent of one tenant.
  • Scoped to one account (one company).
  • Inbox, conversations, contacts, reports, channels.
  • Plus Bloomwire Overview at /app/accounts/:accountId/bloomwire/overview.
Rule of thumb: the URL tells you the layer. /super_admin = platform-wide, Bloomwire team only. /app/accounts/:id = one tenant, that company's staff only.

05 Data ownership

Chatwoot owns the conversation world. Bloomwire owns only the SaaS metadata around it. This boundary is non-negotiable.

💬 Chatwoot owns (source of truth)

All messaging, conversation, contact and channel data lives here.

accounts users account_users inboxes conversations messages contacts teams labels reports / conversation data

🧩 Bloomwire owns (SaaS metadata)

Control-layer metadata that hangs off the tenant. Dashed = planned, not yet built.

business profile metadata plan / status onboarding status onboarding steps tenant health usage analytics audit logs feature flags SaaS-specific permissions
⚠️ Hard rule: Do not duplicate Chatwoot messages, contacts or conversations into Bloomwire tables. Bloomwire references Chatwoot data by its identifiers and reads it live — it never copies it.
🦁 Enterprise boundary & telemetry (ADR 0002): Bloomwire builds only on Chatwoot Community Edition. It must not run Chatwoot Enterprise calling code (under app/enterprise/, gated by channel_voice) in production without a valid Chatwoot Enterprise subscription, and must not depend on the Enterprise Call model, the calls table, or Enterprise voice routes/services. ChatwootHub outbound telemetry is now disabled by default in production (BLOOMWIRE_DISABLE_CHATWOOT_HUB) for privacy / security / SaaS isolation; Enterprise-overlay removal remains parked. Not a licensing bypass, and Enterprise features still require a valid license.
