# 00 — Product Vision

## Target product

**Bloomwire** is an omnichannel customer service SaaS platform built on Chatwoot
Community Edition. It gives businesses one place to handle customer
conversations across WhatsApp, web chat, email, and social channels, and gives
the Bloomwire operator the tools to run it as a multi-tenant SaaS: tenant
management, plans/subscriptions, roles/permissions, audit, usage analytics,
onboarding, and industry presets.

Chatwoot is the conversation engine. Bloomwire is the business/SaaS layer on top.

## Customer types

- **SMB customer service teams** that need affordable omnichannel support
  without stitching together multiple tools.
- **Telecom support teams** (Dialog/Hutch-style) handling high volumes of
  billing, activation, and complaint conversations.
- **Retail / e-commerce businesses** handling order, delivery, and returns
  questions across WhatsApp and chat.
- **Marketing agencies** managing customer conversations on behalf of multiple
  client brands.

## Business problems Bloomwire solves

- **Fragmented channels** — conversations scattered across WhatsApp, email,
  chat, and social with no single view.
- **No SaaS control layer** — Chatwoot alone has no concept of business plans,
  subscriptions, tenant-level onboarding, industry presets, or SaaS-grade audit
  and usage analytics.
- **Weak governance** — businesses need backend-enforced roles, audit trails,
  and usage visibility for compliance and accountability.
- **Slow onboarding** — new businesses need guided setup (channel connection,
  staff invites, industry presets) instead of a blank Chatwoot install.

## Success outcome

- A business can sign up, be provisioned as a tenant, connect a channel (e.g.
  WhatsApp), invite staff, and start handling conversations quickly.
- Bloomwire operators can see all business tenants with SaaS metadata
  (plan, status, industry, onboarding) from a super admin surface.
- Roles and permissions are enforced on the backend; usage and audit data are
  available for governance.
- Chatwoot remains the single source of truth for all conversation data; no
  duplication, no Enterprise dependency.
