# Research — Marketing Agency Use Cases

Marketing agencies manage customer conversations on behalf of multiple client
brands. This shapes Bloomwire's multi-tenant and campaign needs.

## Typical conversation types

- **Campaign responses** — inbound replies to ads/promotions across channels.
- **Lead qualification** — capturing and routing leads to the right brand/team.
- **Client-brand support** — handling support for several brands separately.
- **Reporting to clients** — per-brand volume, response, and outcome metrics.

## Operational characteristics

- **Multi-brand separation** — strict separation between client brands' data and
  conversations.
- **Per-client reporting** — agencies must report results per brand.
- **Campaign-driven spikes** — volume follows campaign launches.
- **Team allocation** — agents/campaign managers may serve multiple clients.

## How Bloomwire (on Chatwoot) fits

- **Multi-tenancy** — one Chatwoot account per brand keeps data separated
  (1 account = 1 tenant). Agencies operate across tenants per their access.
- **Campaign Manager role** for running and monitoring campaign responses.
- **Industry preset (marketing agency)** seeds labels (lead, campaign, brand),
  canned responses, and a per-brand onboarding checklist.
- **Usage analytics** support per-tenant (per-brand) reporting.

## Out of scope (V1)

- Cross-tenant unified agency super-dashboard beyond basic platform views.
- Automated ad-platform integrations.
- Full attribution/billing automation.
