# Research — Omnichannel Customer Service Problems

Practical, real-world problems Bloomwire aims to address. Kept concrete, not
theoretical.

## Common problems

- **Channel fragmentation** — customers reach out via WhatsApp, web chat, email,
  Facebook, and Instagram. Agents juggle multiple tools and lose context.
- **Lost context across channels** — a customer who emailed yesterday and
  WhatsApps today is treated as two separate threads.
- **Slow first response** — without a unified queue, messages sit unassigned and
  response times suffer.
- **No accountability** — hard to see who handled what, when, and how well.
- **Weak reporting** — leaders lack reliable volume, response-time, and
  resolution metrics across channels.
- **Onboarding friction** — connecting channels and configuring teams is
  technical and slow for non-technical businesses.
- **Governance gaps** — limited audit trails and role control for compliance.

## How Chatwoot already helps

- Unified inbox across channels, contact merging, teams, labels, canned
  responses, automation rules, and built-in reporting.

## Where Bloomwire adds value

- SaaS multi-tenancy, plans/subscriptions, guided onboarding, industry presets,
  backend-enforced roles, audit logs, and usage analytics — the business/SaaS
  governance layer Chatwoot CE does not provide out of the box.

## Implication for V1

- Lead with **unified inbox foundation (Chatwoot) + tenant onboarding + WhatsApp
  setup + basic governance**. These hit the highest-pain problems first.
